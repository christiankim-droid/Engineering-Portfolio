# Kelp Desnagger — UCSB Mechanical Engineering (ME153) Junior Capstone

![Kelp Desnagger Poster](images/kelp_desnagger_poster.heif)

## Overview
Fishing lures frequently become tangled in thick kelp stalks when angling near ocean kelp beds. Pulling on the line often snaps it, leading to lost tackle, unrecovered gear, and marine pollution. 

The **Kelp Desnagger** is a compact, reusable motorized device designed for recreational anglers fishing from shore, piers, boats, or kayaks. It attaches directly to a tensioned fishing line and travels down to the snagged lure, where a cutting mechanism severs the surrounding kelp stalks to free the tackle without breaking the main fishing line.

---

## Technical & Engineering Highlights

* **Analytical Modeling:** Applied capstan tension friction equations ($T_2 = T_1 e^{\mu \alpha}$) across custom rubber grommet wheel pulleys to determine optimal line wrapping, motor torque requirements ($\tau_{\text{required}} = 26.2\text{ mN}\cdot\text{m}$ vs. $\tau_{\text{motor}} = 98.11\text{ mN}\cdot\text{m}$), and shaft resultant force loads ($F_r = 13.0\text{ N}$).
* **Empirical Testing & Characterization:**
  * **Line Angle vs. Velocity:** Characterized travel speeds across line angles from $26^\circ$ to $44^\circ$, verifying consistent traversal along tensioned lines.
  * **Payload & Mass Sensitivity:** Mapped average travel velocity against device mass, demonstrating reliable line movement up to maximum motor stall thresholds under full battery load.
* **Mechanism & Prototype:** Built a lightweight motorized chassis integrated with custom line-tracking wheels and a kelp-cutting assembly.

---

## Key Results & Future Work

* **Performance:** Prototype successfully traversed tensioned fishing line directly to the snag site. Performance peaked when line tension was kept tight and held at an angle, successfully demonstrating proof-of-concept for lure retrieval.
* **Next Iterations:**
  * Waterproofing housing for submerged saltwater exposure.
  * Design for Manufacturing (DFM) optimization for injection molding and mass production.
